Post-Graduation Planning

Planning Your Next Move After Graduation

Whether entering the workforce immediately or preparing for higher education, here is how to evaluate your options without career paralysis.

Direct Job Market Entry

For graduates eager to start earning and building real-world experience. Focus shifts from academic credentials to proof-of-work portfolios, cold outreach, and ATS resume optimization.

  • Bypass standard job portals via direct manager outreach
  • Build 2 verified portfolio projects (GitHub / Power BI)
  • Master the STAR behavioral interview method

Higher Education & Specialization

Evaluating MBA, Master of Business Analytics, specialized certifications (CFA, FRM), or postgraduate diplomas based on your financial ROI and timeline goals.

  • Assess work-ex vs. immediate post-grad timing
  • Target high-ROI quantitative & analytics degrees
  • Align professional certifications with target industries
