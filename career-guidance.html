Direct Entry

Campus Placement Strategy

The most frictionless route. Pre-final year internships dictate who gets shortlisted. Focus on building 2 high-quality projects rather than 10 generic ones.

Fastest Growing

Skill-Up & Industry Pivot

Degrees matter less than verifiable skills. A rigorous 3–6 month certification allows arts/commerce grads to pivot into tech.

Highest Hiring Interest

Data Analytics & AI

Pair Python, SQL, and Power BI. This is the fastest way to make a non-technical degree immediately attractive to tech recruiters.

The Multiplier Effect

Core Degree + Applied AI

A Mechanical Engineer who knows how to train AI models for predictive maintenance. This combo is worth 3x the base degree.

Step 1

The 6-Second ATS Resume

Recruiters scan for exactly 6 seconds. Single-column format, zero progress bars. Lead with hard metrics (e.g., "Increased sales by 15%").

Step 2

Bypassing the Job Portal

Cold applying has a ~2% success rate. Learn to use LinkedIn boolean searches to find hiring managers and send highly-targeted cold outreach.
