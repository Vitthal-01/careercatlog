Career advice hasn't kept pace with how fast careers are changing.

Most career guidance still reads like it was written for a job market from a decade ago. Meanwhile, the skills that matter, the tools people use daily, and the shape of entry-level work are shifting faster than most advice can track — largely because of AI.

CareerCatalog exists to close that gap. We cover two things properly, instead of many things poorly: practical career guidance for students and early-career professionals, and sharp analysis on where AI and content are actually headed — not hype, not filler.

If you're trying to figure out what skills are worth your time, what the job market actually looks like right now, or how AI is reshaping the work you're training for, that's what this is for.

What we won't do

Publish generic listicles, recycle the same "top 10 skills" advice everyone else has, or chase trends without checking if they're actually true.

What we will do

Write things worth your time — specific, current, and honest about what's actually useful versus what just sounds impressive.

Start with what matters to you right now.
