Frameworks Over Fluff

Enterprise Prompt Architecture

We teach the exact, repeatable mental models used by Fortune 500 business analysts and product managers.

01 / C-T-C-F FRAMEWORK

Context, Task, Constraint, Format

Stop asking open questions. Define the exact corporate persona, give precise background boundaries, enforce anti-hallucination constraints, and specify the markdown table output.

02 / FEW-SHOT REASONING

Exemplar-Driven Prompting

Provide 2–3 exact golden examples of your team's standard reports before demanding generation. This eliminates 95% of formatting errors on the first pass.

03 / CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT (CoT)

Step-by-Step Logic Gates

Force the language model to write its intermediate calculations, data checks, and reasoning steps before producing the final executive conclusion.
