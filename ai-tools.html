How to Talk to AI Like a Pro

Remember these 3 simple rules whenever you ask an AI tool for help to get the best results every time.

RULE 01

Give Clear Background

Tell the AI who you are and what you need. For example: "I am a B.Com student preparing an application email for an internship."

RULE 02

Be Specific on Format

Tell it how you want the answer structured—whether as bullet points, a short paragraph, or a clean table.

RULE 03

Ask Follow-Up Questions

If the first answer is too long or formal, simply reply: "Make it shorter and more friendly" just like chatting with a friend.
