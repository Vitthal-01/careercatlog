Explore your path

Wherever you are, there's a genuine next step

Pick the stage you're at. Every option below is a real path Indian students are actually choosing right now — not a textbook list.

Most chosen · PCM

Engineering & tech (JEE / state CETs)

Still the default for PCM students. Within B.Tech, AI, Data Science, and core CS branches are pulling ahead of traditional streams in recruiter demand.

High demand · PCB

Medical & allied health (NEET)

MBBS seats are limited and competitive, so more PCB students are seriously considering nursing, physiotherapy, and other allied health degrees as a faster, genuine route in.

Dependable · Commerce

Commerce, CA & BBA

B.Com and BBA remain solid choices, and CA is still one of India's most respected qualifications — tough exams, but strong long-term payoff.

Fast-growing · Arts

Design, law & humanities

UCEED/NID for design and CLAT for law are drawing more students each year, as families see these pay well without needing PCM.

Direct entry

Campus placement

Still the most common route straight into a core job — the internship you did in your pre-final year often decides which companies shortlist you.

Long-term

Higher studies — M.Tech, MBA, MS

CAT for MBA, GATE for M.Tech, or GRE/IELTS for studying abroad. Worth it when it clearly changes your role or salary band, not just for the degree.

Stability + prestige

Government exams

UPSC, banking (IBPS), and SSC still attract huge numbers of graduates every year for the job security and social respect they carry.

Fastest-growing

Skill-up & pivot

A 3–6 month certification in data, product, or digital marketing is helping graduates from any stream move into roles that don't match their original degree.

Highest hiring interest

Data analytics & AI

Python, SQL, and Power BI through Google's or Coursera's data certificates — one of the fastest ways to make a non-tech degree tech-relevant.

In demand · content

Digital marketing

Google and Meta certifications, specialising in either performance marketing or SEO — a genuine path for students who like content but want measurable results.

High-paying · tech

Cloud computing

AWS, Azure, or GCP associate-level certifications — increasingly asked for even in roles that aren't purely "cloud engineer."

Steady growth

Product & finance

PM certifications for product roles, and CFA or NISM for a serious finance track — both open doors that a general degree alone often doesn't.

The real trend

AI layered on your core degree

A mechanical engineer who understands AI in manufacturing, or a law student who understands AI and data privacy — the combination is worth more than either skill alone.

Business-facing

Data + business sense

Companies need people who can read a dashboard and explain what to actually do about it — not just build the dashboard.

Content careers

Writing + marketing know-how

Good writers who also understand SEO and basic analytics get picked over writers who only write — this pairing is now a genuine hiring filter.

Builder's edge

Design + no-code tools

Students who can design and actually ship a working app or website using no-code tools stand out in internship applications, without needing a full dev background.

Step 1

A resume recruiters read

One page, simple format, numbers over adjectives. Most freshers lose the recruiter in the first ten seconds with a cluttered layout.

Step 2

Where to actually look

Naukri, LinkedIn, and Internshala for volume — but a referral from someone you know still converts far better than a cold application.

Step 3

Interview prep that works

Research the company, prepare real examples using a simple situation-action-result structure, and prepare two honest questions to ask them back.

Step 4

Turn internships into offers

Treat your internship as an extended interview — most pre-placement offers go to interns who asked good questions and delivered on small tasks reliably.
