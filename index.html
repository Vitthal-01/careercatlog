YOUR QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions.

Here are answers to common questions about our resume services.

What is a professional resume?

A professional resume is a document that presents your education, work experience, skills, achievements and qualifications to employers.

What is an ATS-friendly resume?

ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. It is software many companies use to organise and review job applications. An ATS-friendly resume uses clear headings, readable formatting and relevant job-related keywords.

Can you make a resume for freshers?

Yes. We create resumes for freshers using their education, internships, projects, skills, certifications and achievements.

Can you improve my existing resume?

Yes. We can help rewrite and improve your existing resume to make it clearer, more professional and relevant to your target job.

Do you create resumes for experienced professionals?

Yes. We create resumes for professionals across different industries, experience levels and job roles.

How do I order a resume?

Select a design from our collection, click on it and share your contact details. Our team will contact you to discuss your requirements.

Will you write the resume for me?

Yes. Our team will create your resume based on the information and documents you share with us.
